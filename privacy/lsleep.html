Privacy Policy for LSleep

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how LSleep ("we", "our", or "the App") respects and protects your privacy. LSleep is designed to offer calm, uninterrupted sleep sounds without collecting any personal data.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

LSleep does not collect, track, transmit, or monetize any personal information, location details, device identifiers, or usage logs. You can use all audio tracks and features without creating an account or logging in.

2. Audio Engine & Local Device Playback

All sleep tracks, frequency filters, and sound synthesis algorithms run locally on your device. The App does not stream audio from external servers or monitor your listening history.

3. Preferences & Timer Settings

Your user preferences—including selected sound tracks, timer duration, volume settings, and theme choices—are stored strictly on your device using Apple's local UserDefaults storage. This data never leaves your device.

4. Third-Party Trackers & Advertising

LSleep contains no advertisements, no tracking SDKs, and no third-party analytics frameworks. No external servers are maintained to collect or process user information.

5. Children's Privacy

Because no personal data is collected from any user, LSleep does not collect or request information from children under the age of 13.
