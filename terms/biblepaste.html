2. License Grant, Lifetime Access & Device Pairing

  • Perpetual Lifetime License (Up to 3 Devices): A purchase grants a perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable license for the chosen direct edition. A Mac license activates up to three Macs at a time, and a Windows license activates up to three Windows PCs at a time. Each operating system requires a separate purchase.
  • Cryptographic Device Verification: During activation, a one-way SHA-256 cryptographic hash derived from the machine's hardware identifier is securely transmitted to Cloudflare (US) servers solely to maintain active seat counts. Internet access is required once during the initial activation of each computer.
  • Device Migration & Remote Unbinding: You can deregister an active computer at any time via the App Preferences or tray/menubar menu to free an activation slot for another computer running the same operating system. If an activated computer was formatted, lost, or broken, our support team can remotely release the inactive device slot upon request.
